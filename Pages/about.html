About Cook n Flush

Company History

Cook n Flush is a local store that sells bathroom and kitchen appliances and was established back in the year 2015 in Cape Town, South Africa. This business’s focus is specializing in selling in the installations of kitchen appliances and bathroom appliances to its customers, showing different intricate design variations of the appliances.

Vision statement

Our vision is To become Cape Town’s most recognizable and used business in products involving kitchen and bathroom appliances, and one of the best online services for households in the country.
