Project Overview

Cook n Flush is a local store that sells bathroom and kitchen appliances and was established back in
the year 2015 in Cape Town, South Africa. This business’s focus is specializing in selling in the
installations of kitchen appliances and bathroom appliances to its customers, showing different
intricate design variations of the appliances.

Website goals and objectives

Its goal is to become Cape Town’s most recognizable and used business in products involving kitchen and
bathroom appliances, and one of the best online services for households in the country. It also wants 
to expand on its business service and gain more exposure to the public while attracting more
customers.

Key Features and Functionality

Search bar: a function which searchers for what ever the product the user types in.  
Link to products: there are highlighted links to view the details of the product
Register and login form: input forms for when logging into an account or for when registering for the first time to the website
Enquiry form: a form in which the user is able to send message enquiry to the business organization of the website
Google map: a feature in which displays the google map location of the business.
Wishlist: a button that adds a product into their wishlist
Filter: a filter function where it displays products under their respective category for users to look for
Navigation tab: tab which links to other pages to access to.

CSS Styling

Typography

Applied Arial font into the website for the normal text that displays the information on the business, products and when user types into the forms in the website. The headings are all in verdana and are also bold in order to make them standout on the information below them to show what they are about.

Styles

I have applied block style features making them separate and not make it lack a sense of style and bland. Applied colors related to the theme of what the website is for and relating to the organization, making the website seem more presentable and decorative. There is also responsive designs added into the CSS file for when the mouse cursor or selector is on a picture or is on the navigation tabs making the website responsive.
