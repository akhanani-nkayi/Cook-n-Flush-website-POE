Cook n Flush is a local store that sells bathroom and kitchen appliances and was established back in
the year 2015 in Cape Town, South Africa. This business’s focus is specializing in selling in the
installations of kitchen appliances and bathroom appliances to its customers, showing different
intricate design variations of the appliances.

Goals
Its goal is to become Cape Town’s most recognizable and used business in products involving kitchen and
bathroom appliances, and one of the best online services for households in the country. It also wants 
to expand on its business service and gain more exposure to the public while attracting more
customers.

Features and Functionality
It has a search bar, links to other pages based on the clicked product, has a submit button for when inputing
information and messages on enquiry page.
